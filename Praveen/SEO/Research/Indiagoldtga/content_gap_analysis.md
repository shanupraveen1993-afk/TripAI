## Key Insights

1. **Indiagold.co strengths:**
   - Bank-specific gold loan content (SBI, Canara, IIFL)
   - City-specific gold rates (Surat, Chennai, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Indore)
   - Gold loan calculator

2. **Major gaps:**
   - No customer portal/LMS (competitors getting 400k+ traffic)
   - Missing state-wise gold rates (only city-level)
   - No branch locator tool
   - Limited online application/payment functionality pages
   - No historical gold price tracking
   - Missing business loan related content (Udyam)
   - No personal loan product offering

3. **Quick wins:**
   - Create state-wise gold rate pages (Rajasthan, AP, Karnataka, etc.)
   - Develop branch locator functionality
   - Add historical gold prices chart/tool
   - Create educational blog content (Tola, 24k vs 22k)
   - Expand to more city-specific landing pages (Nagpur, Pune, etc.)

4. **Long-term investments:**
   - Build customer portal/LMS system
   - Develop online payment infrastructure
   - Add personal loan product line
   - Create career/hiring section
