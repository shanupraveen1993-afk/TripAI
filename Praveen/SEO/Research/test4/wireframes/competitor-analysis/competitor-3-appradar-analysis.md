# App Radar - Keyword Intelligence Tool Analysis

## Overview
App Radar positions itself as an AI-powered ASO platform with a comprehensive keyword intelligence tool featuring a "45M keyword database" and emphasizing keyword discovery, tracking, and optimization for app visibility.

**Core Value Proposition:** "Find the relevant keywords that drive app visibility"

**Key Differentiators:**
- AI-powered keyword suggestions and semantically related terms
- 45 million keyword database
- Automatic keyword translation for international markets
- Real-time global ranking updates across multiple countries
- Integrated ASO effort effectiveness tracking
