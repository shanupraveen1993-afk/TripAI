## TIER 1 PRIORITY: Launch Within 3-6 Months

### **1. NYSE FANG+ ETF (International Tech ETF)**

**Priority Score: 9.2/10** ⭐ HIGHEST

#### **Demand Analysis**
- **Primary Keyword:** "mirae asset nyse fang+ etf" - 8,100 searches
- **Secondary Keywords:**
  - "mafang etf" - 8,100
  - "fang etf" - 1,300
  - "faang etf" - 320
- **Total Monthly Traffic Potential:** 15,000+ visitors
- **Current Competitor Traffic:** Mirae Asset getting 15,495/month

**Scoring:**
- Search Demand: 10/10 (Highest single opportunity)
- Competition: 9/10 (Only 1 major competitor - Mirae Asset)
- Regulatory Feasibility: 7/10 (International investment, LRS compliance)
- Operational Complexity: 7/10 (Fund-of-fund structure required)
- Strategic Fit: 10/10 (Perfect for Zerodha's tech-savvy audience)

#### **Market Opportunity**
- **Untapped Demand:** Tech-savvy Indian investors want US tech stock exposure
- **Competitor Monopoly:** Only Mirae Asset offers this currently
- **Brand Alignment:** Meta, Apple, Netflix, Google = Zerodha user profile
- **USD Hedge:** Rupee depreciation protection appeals to HNIs

#### **Implementation Strategy**
1. **Structure:** Fund-of-Fund (FoF) investing in NYSE FANG+ Index
2. **Underlying Index:** NYSE FANG+ Index (10 tech stocks)
3. **Components:** Meta, Apple, Nvidia, Netflix, Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, Tesla, Broadcom, AMD
4. **Expense Ratio Target:** 0.75-1.00% (competitive with Mirae)
5. **Minimum Investment:** ₹500-1000 per unit

#### **Go-to-Market**
- **Launch Timeline:** 3-4 months (FoF structure faster than direct)
- **Marketing Angle:** "Invest in the world's top tech companies"
- **Target Audience:**
  - Age 25-40
  - Tech professionals
  - HNI investors
  - Long-term wealth creators
- **Educational Content:** Create series on US tech investing
- **PR Strategy:** Position as "first from new-age AMC"

#### **Revenue Projection**
- **Target AUM (Year 1):** ₹500-750 crores
- **Management Fee:** 0.85%
- **Annual Revenue:** ₹4.25-6.38 crores

#### **Risks & Mitigation**
- **Currency Risk:** Clearly communicate in investor education
- **Regulatory Changes:** LRS limit changes - monitor policy
- **Tech Sector Volatility:** Emphasize long-term horizon (7+ years)
- **Competition:** Mirae has first-mover advantage - counter with lower TER

**RECOMMENDATION:** **LAUNCH IMMEDIATELY** - Highest ROI opportunity

- Aligns with Zerodha's modern brand
- Targets HNI & sophisticated investors

### **2. Build Complete Sectoral Suite**

- Easy to understand for retail

- Thought leadership positioning

- Create educational content BEFORE product launch
- Build anticipation and waitlists
- SEO benefits for long-term traffic
- Establish expertise

### **5. Leverage Zerodha Ecosystem**
- Cross-promote on Kite, Coin apps
- In-app notifications to 1M+ users
- Zero distribution cost advantage
- Fastest go-to-market

## Risk Mitigation

### **Regulatory Risks**
- **International ETFs:** LRS limit changes, SEBI policy shifts
- **Mitigation:** Diversify with domestic products, FoF structure flexibility

### **Market Risks**
- **Tech Volatility:** FANG+ concentrated exposure
- **Mitigation:** Clear investor education, long-term positioning

### **Competition Risks**
- **Me-too Products:** Others may copy after successful launch
- **Mitigation:** First-mover advantage, lowest TER strategy, brand loyalty

### **Execution Risks**
- **Regulatory Delays:** Approvals may take longer
- **Mitigation:** Parallel processing, experienced compliance team
