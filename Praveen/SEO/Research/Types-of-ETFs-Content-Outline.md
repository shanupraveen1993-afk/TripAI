## **Section 4: Tax Implications by ETF Type**
**H2:** Understanding Tax Treatment of Different ETF Types

**Content (300 words):**

**Post-Budget 2023 Changes:**

### **Equity ETFs (Equity-oriented)**
- **Short-term (< 1 year):** 20% (STT paid)
- **Long-term (> 1 year):** 12.5% on gains above ₹1.25 lakh
- No indexation benefit

### **Debt ETFs**
- **All gains:** Taxed as per income tax slab
- No distinction between short/long term
- No indexation benefit (removed from 2023)

### **Gold/Silver ETFs**
- **Short-term (< 3 years):** As per income tax slab
- **Long-term (> 3 years):** 12.5%
- Indexation benefit removed

### **International ETFs**
- Treated as **debt funds**
- All gains: As per income tax slab
- No indexation

### **Tax Harvesting Strategies:**
- Book LTCG up to ₹1.25 lakh yearly (tax-free)
- Offset gains with losses
- Rebalancing opportunities
